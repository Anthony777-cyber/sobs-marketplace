S.O.B.S. Bulk Spreadsheet Builder

1. Extract the downloaded ZIP.
2. Double-click "Install SOBS BSB.cmd". It installs the app and blank Excel template in your local app folder, creates the SOBS desktop icon, and opens the builder.
3. The builder creates a populated .xlsx file with embedded photos. Use the blank template only if you prefer to enter listings manually.

The builder works locally in your browser and does not upload information from your computer. To remove it, delete the "SOBS BSB" folder from your local app data and remove the desktop shortcut.
