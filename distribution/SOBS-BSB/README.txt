S.O.B.S. BSB v2

1. Extract the downloaded ZIP.
2. Double-click "Install SOBS BSB.cmd". It installs BSB v2 and the blank Excel template in your local app folder, creates the SOBS desktop icon, and opens the uploader.
3. BSB v2 applies the updated Sifter classification flow to the batch, then creates a populated .xlsx file with embedded photos. Use the blank template only if you prefer to enter listings manually.

The uploader works locally in your browser and does not upload information from your computer. To remove it, delete the "SOBS BSB" folder from your local app data and remove the desktop shortcut.
